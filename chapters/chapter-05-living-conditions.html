Access & Quality of Life
AI Insight — Phase 4

Nearly 6 in 10 Hondurans live below the poverty line, and over a quarter face extreme poverty. Yet basic infrastructure tells a more nuanced story: electricity reaches 91% of homes and clean water 87%, suggesting the gap is less about physical access and more about income. Child malnutrition at 23% remains a critical concern — a lagging indicator that reflects years of compounding deprivation.
